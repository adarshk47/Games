Install on iPhone / iPad

No App Store needed – add Master G to your home screen:

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Play now.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow ↑) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Master G from your home screen – it runs full screen like an app.

🤖 Android

Get the full app with widgets and shortcuts:

  1. Open Master G on Google Play and tap Install.
  2. Or play right here in Chrome: tap Play now → menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
