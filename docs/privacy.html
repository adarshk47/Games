Privacy Policy – Master G

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Master G ("the app") is a collection of brain and puzzle games published by Adarsh Kumar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Local profile: the display name, country and language you choose, and an optional PIN (stored only as a salted hash) and fingerprint setting. These stay on your device.
  • Game data: your progress, stars, scores, coins and settings. Stored on your device; if you sign in, a copy is saved to our cloud database so it can be restored.
  • Account (optional sign-in): if you sign in with Google or email/password, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from Firebase Authentication.
  • Leaderboards (signed-in players only): your display name, country and best scores are visible to other players.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.
  • Invites: if you open the app from an invite link, the Google Play Install Referrer tells us which player invited you so both of you can receive a coin bonus.

2. How we use information

  • To run the games, save your progress and restore it on a new device.
  • To show leaderboards (global and by country).
  • To give rewards such as coins and invite bonuses.
  • To show ads that keep the app free, and to send the optional daily reminder notification you can turn off in Settings.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Play services – policies.google.com/privacy

Users in the European Economic Area are asked for ad consent through Google's consent form.

4. Data security and storage

Data sent to our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud data is stored by Google Firebase. Access rules make sure each player can read and change only their own saved data.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all saved data at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. You can also request deletion on the web: Delete your Master G account. Local data is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
