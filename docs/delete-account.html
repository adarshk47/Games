Delete your Master G account

Master G is published by Adarsh Kumar. You can delete your account and its data in two ways.

Option 1 – In the app (instant)

  1. Open Master G and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This immediately deletes your cloud save, your leaderboard entries, invite records and your sign-in account, and removes the data from your phone.

Option 2 – By email

Send an email to aiforsmart@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Master G account" from the email address you used to sign in (or include your Google account email). We will delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email, user ID).
  • Your cloud save: game progress, stars, scores, coins and settings.
  • Your leaderboard entries and invite records.

What may be kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Ad-related data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's own policies. Backups in Google Firebase are overwritten within about 30 days.
