Privacy Policy for Memory Puzzle

Last updated: March 30, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Memory Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application Memory Puzzle (Package Name: com.memorypuzzle.app).

1. Information Collection and Use

Memory Puzzle is designed as a privacy-first, offline-capable puzzle application.

A. Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

B. Local Data Storage

The App uses local storage (SharedPreferences) on your device to save:

  • Game progress and levels unlocked
  • High scores and statistics
  • App preferences (such as audio/theme settings)

This data is stored strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

C. Biometric Authentication

If you choose to use biometric authentication (e.g., Fingerprint or Face Unlock) within the app:

  • Biometric authentication is handled entirely by your device's native operating system.
  • We do not have access to, collect, or store your biometric data or fingerprints.

2. Third-Party Services & Permissions

The App requests minimal permissions required for core functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Standard network access capability.
  • android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC: Allows optional local biometric authentication on your device.

We do not integrate third-party tracking, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks that gather user data.

3. Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for users of all ages, including children. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted to external servers, your data remains secure on your physical device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us.
