🍂 Season by season
☀️
General tips

Summer: stay cool

  • Drink water, chaas, nimbu-paani through the day
  • Eat light – fruits, salads, curd rice
  • Avoid the midday sun and very spicy food
🌧️
General tips

Monsoon: care for digestion

  • Drink boiled or filtered warm water
  • Eat freshly cooked, warm food; skip street food
  • Add ginger and jeera to cooking
❄️
General tips

Winter: warmth and strength

  • Eat seasonal vegetables, til and gud in small amounts
  • Keep active – a morning walk in the sun
  • Oil massage before a warm bath
