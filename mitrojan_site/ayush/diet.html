❓ FAQ
Is it only for vegetarians?

No. Plans are made for vegetarian, eggetarian, non-vegetarian, Jain and vegan food.

When will I get my diet chart?

After your call. The clinic will tell you the exact timeline when it confirms your booking.

Can my whole family use it?

Ask about the Family Yearly Care plan – it includes diet guidance for the family kitchen.

Do I have to stop my medicines?

No. Never stop or change a medicine without your doctor. The diet works alongside your treatment.
