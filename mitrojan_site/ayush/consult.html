❓ FAQ
Will I get medicines?

Only if the doctor decides you need them. Often food, routine and home care come first. Any prescription carries the doctor's name, degree and registration number.

Can I share my reports?

Yes. Keep them ready for the call; the clinic will tell you how to share them after confirming your booking.

Can I book for a family member?

Yes. Enter their name and mention their age in the note. For babies and serious problems, the doctor may advise an in-person visit.

Do I have to stop my current medicines?

No. Never stop or change a medicine without your doctor. Tell Vaidya Dolly Gupta everything you are taking.
