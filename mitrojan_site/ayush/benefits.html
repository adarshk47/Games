🌱 Benefits

Why people are choosing Ayurveda again

A 5,000-year-old idea that feels very new: look after the whole person – food, routine, sleep and mind – before small problems become big ones.

🧬Prakriti

Made for you, not for everyone

Ayurveda looks at your prakriti – your natural body and mind type – so food and routine advice fits you, instead of one plan for all.

Try today: notice which foods leave you light and which leave you heavy.
🔥Agni

Happy digestion

Good health starts with agni – your digestion. Eating at the right time and in the right amount matters as much as what you eat.

Try today: make lunch your biggest meal.
🌅Dinacharya

A daily routine that works

Waking, eating and sleeping at regular times keeps your body clock steady – energy, mood and digestion all improve.

Try today: sleep and wake at the same time.
🍂Ritucharya

Live with the seasons

Summer, monsoon and winter each need different food and habits. Small seasonal changes help you stay well all year.

Try today: eat fruit and vegetables that are in season.
🧘Yoga

Mind + body together

Stress, sleep and breathing affect your body. Ayurveda treats them as part of care, not as extras.

Try today: 5 minutes of slow breathing before bed.
🛡️Swasthya

Prevention first

The aim is to stay healthy, not only to treat illness – with food and lifestyle first, and medicine only when a doctor advises.

Try today: book a yearly health check-up, even if you feel fine.
🌬️🔥🌍 Doshas

Vata, Pitta, Kapha – in simple words

Everyone has all three. Your own mix is your prakriti – a Vaidya can help you understand yours.

🌬️

Vata

Air + space

Light, quick, creative and full of ideas.

When out of balance: dryness, gas, worry, poor sleep.

🔥

Pitta

Fire + water

Sharp, focused, warm and driven.

When out of balance: acidity, heat, irritability.

🌍

Kapha

Earth + water

Calm, steady, strong and caring.

When out of balance: heaviness, weight gain, low energy.

⏰ Dinacharya

A day, the Ayurveda way

A simple routine for most healthy adults. Shift the times to suit your work.

6:00Wake up, a glass of warm water
6:30Walk, yoga or stretching – 30 minutes
8:00Light, fresh breakfast
1:00Lunch – the biggest meal of the day
4:30A fruit or roasted chana, herbal tea
7:30Early, light dinner and a short walk
10:00Screens off, sleep
🤔 Myths vs facts

Let's bust a few myths

Ayurveda is only herbs and kadha.

It is a full system – food, routine, sleep, movement, and medicine when needed.

Ayurvedic means no side effects.

Any medicine can have side effects. Take Ayurvedic medicines only on a qualified Vaidya's advice.

You must choose Ayurveda or allopathy.

They can work together. Always tell both your doctors what you are taking.
