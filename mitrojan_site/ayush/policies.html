How we handle your data, your consultation and refunds. Last updated: October 2026.

🔒 Privacy policy

What we collect: your name, mobile number, chosen doctor, date and time, language, and the short note you type when booking or requesting a diet plan.

Why: only to arrange and run your consultation and to contact you about it.

Who can see it: only the clinic team, through a protected login. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

How long we keep it: only as long as needed for your care, and as long as the law requires.

Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to aiforsmart@gmail.com.

Questions or complaints: write to aiforsmart@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

📹 Teleconsultation consent

  • Consultations are online video calls of about 15 minutes, for patients in India.
  • The doctor may not be able to examine you physically and may advise an in-person visit or tests.
  • Advice and any prescription depend on the doctor's judgement; a prescription is not guaranteed.
  • You share correct information about your health and current medicines.
  • This service is not for emergencies – call 112.

💸 Cancellation & refund

  • You can reschedule or cancel for free before the clinic confirms your slot. After that, the clinic will tell you the rule when it confirms.
  • If the doctor cannot attend, you get a new slot or a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method; the clinic will tell you the expected time.
  • Plans are one-time and prepaid. There is no automatic renewal.

📄 Terms of use

  • Information on this website is general education, not medical advice for you.
  • Do not stop or change any medicine without asking your doctor.
  • Product and plan details and prices may change; the final details are confirmed before you pay.
