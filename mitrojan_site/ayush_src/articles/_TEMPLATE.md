Yahan se article likhna shuru kariye. Ek khaali line chhodne par naya paragraph banta hai.

## Yeh ek heading hai

Heading ke neeche apni baat likhiye. **Bold** karna ho to shabd ke dono taraf do-do star lagaiye.

- Yeh ek point hai
- Yeh doosra point hai
- Har point "- " (dash aur space) se shuru kariye

## Doosri heading

Kisi page ka link dena ho to aise likhiye: [diet plan dekhiye](diet.html)
