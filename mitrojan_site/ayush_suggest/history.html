📜 History

The story of Ayurveda

One of the world's oldest systems of health care – born in India, written down by great Vaidyas, and still part of millions of Indian homes today.

आयुAyu = life+वेदVeda = knowledge=Knowledge of life
🕰️ Timeline

From the Vedas to today

Dates for ancient texts are scholars' estimates and vary.

🕉️3,000+ years ago

Vedic roots

The Atharvaveda contains hymns about healing plants and remedies – the early seeds of Ayurveda.

🪔Tradition

Bhagwan Dhanvantari

Revered as the god of Ayurveda and the physician of the gods. His day, Dhanteras, is now National Ayurveda Day.

📖c. 2nd century BCE – 2nd century CE

Charaka Samhita

The great text of kaya chikitsa (internal medicine) – diet, daily routine, diagnosis and treatment.

🔪Early centuries CE (older roots)

Sushruta Samhita

The classic of surgery. It describes many surgical instruments and procedures, including nose reconstruction – Sushruta is often called the father of surgery.

✍️c. 7th century CE

Ashtanga Hridaya

Vagbhata brought the knowledge together in clear verse. With Charaka and Sushruta, it forms the Brihat Trayi – the great trio.

🌏c. 8th century CE onwards

Ayurveda travels

Ayurvedic texts were translated into Persian and Arabic, and the tradition influenced healing in Sri Lanka, Tibet and South-East Asia.

🌿7th – 16th century

The Laghu Trayi

Madhava Nidana (diagnosis), Sharangadhara Samhita (medicine making, pulse reading) and Bhavaprakasha (herbs and food) – the lesser trio.

🏛️1970

Recognised by law

The Indian Medicine Central Council Act set standards for Ayurveda education and practice.

🇮🇳2014

Ministry of AYUSH

India created a full ministry for Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa and Homoeopathy.

🎉2016

National Ayurveda Day

Celebrated every year on Dhanteras (Dhanvantari Jayanti).

⚖️2020

NCISM

The National Commission for Indian System of Medicine became the regulator for Ayurveda education and practice.

🌐2022

WHO centre in India

The foundation stone of the WHO Global Centre for Traditional Medicine was laid in Jamnagar, Gujarat.
