🌱 Why Ayurveda?

Benefits of Ayurveda, in plain words

An old system with a very modern idea: care that fits you, your food and your day.

Prakriti🧬

Made for you, not for everyone

Ayurveda looks at your prakriti – your natural body and mind type – so food and routine advice fits you, instead of one plan for all.

Agni🔥

Happy digestion

Strong focus on agni (digestion): eating at the right time, in the right amount, food that suits you.

Dinacharya🌅

A daily routine that works

Wake early, regular meals, sleep on time – small habits that add up to a big difference.

Ritucharya🍂

Live with the seasons

Change food and routine as the season changes – summer, monsoon and winter each need something different.

Yoga🧘

Mind + body together

Breathing, yoga, sleep and stress are part of care – not an extra.

Swasthya🛡️

Prevention first

The aim is to stay well, not only to treat illness – with food and lifestyle first, and medicine only when your doctor advises.

🥗 Eat right

What should you eat? Keep it simple.

General guidance for healthy adults in India. Your doctor can personalise it for you.

Your balanced thali

  • ½ Vegetables & saladSabzi, salad, leafy greens – different colours.
  • ¼ Whole grainsRoti, millets (ragi, bajra, jowar), brown or regular rice in small portions.
  • ¼ ProteinDal, rajma, chana, paneer, curd, sprouts – or egg and fish if you eat them.
  • + Water, fruit, a little gheeA seasonal fruit, chaas or curd, and enough water through the day.

Start light and fresh

Within 1–2 hours of waking

  • A glass of warm water
  • Poha or upma with vegetables
  • Besan or moong chilla
  • Idli-sambar or oats
  • Sprouts with a seasonal fruit

The main meal of the day

Around 12:30–2 pm

  • Roti or rice with dal
  • One seasonal sabzi
  • Salad – cucumber, carrot, onion
  • Curd or chaas
  • Eat slowly and chew well

A smart snack

Around 4:30–6 pm

  • Roasted chana or makhana
  • A fruit or a handful of nuts
  • Herbal or ginger tea, less sugar
  • Skip biscuits, chips and samosa most days

Early and light

By 8 pm, 2–3 hours before sleep

  • Khichdi with a little ghee
  • Dal-roti with sabzi
  • Vegetable soup or daliya
  • A short walk after dinner

✅ Eat more

  • Seasonal vegetables
  • Fresh fruits
  • Millets
  • Dal & sprouts
  • Curd & chaas
  • Nuts & seeds
  • Haldi, jeera, ajwain, adrak
  • Home-cooked, warm food

🚫 Eat less

  • Sugar & sweets
  • Maida & bakery
  • Deep-fried snacks
  • Packaged & junk food
  • Cold drinks
  • Too much salt & pickle
  • Late-night eating

Diabetes, BP, kidney or thyroid problems, pregnancy or allergies? Your diet needs a doctor's advice – please don't follow general tips alone.
