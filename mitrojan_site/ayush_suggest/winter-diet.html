Your winter diet plan

Warm, simple Indian food for the cold months – a sample day you can follow, easy swaps, and a lifestyle that keeps you light and active.

PGVaidya Pooja GuptaDiet, Nutrition & Lifestyle Management · 6 min read

In winter your digestion is strong, so this is a good season to eat nourishing food – but in the right amount and at the right time. Here is a sample day for a healthy adult. Change the quantities to suit your hunger, work and weight goal.

🗓️ A sample winter day

Early morning6:30 – 7:00
  • 1 glass warm water
  • 4–5 soaked almonds
Breakfast8:00 – 9:00
  • Vegetable daliya, or methi-besan chilla, or bajra-moong chilla
  • 1 amla or an orange
Mid-morning11:00
  • A guava or orange, or a small handful of roasted peanuts
Lunch – main meal1:00 – 2:00
  • Bajra or makka roti (or rice) with dal
  • Sarson or palak saag with a little ghee
  • Salad – mooli, gajar, kheera
  • Curd or chaas – at lunch only
Evening4:30 – 5:30
  • Adrak-tulsi tea with less sugar
  • Roasted chana or makhana – or 1 small til-gud laddoo
Dinner – light and early7:30 – 8:00
  • Vegetable soup first
  • Moong dal khichdi, or 2 roti with sabzi
Bedtime (optional)9:30
  • A cup of warm haldi milk

🔁 Easy winter swaps

Cold drinks→Warm soup or kadha
Biscuits and namkeen→Roasted makhana or chana
Maida paratha→Bajra or methi roti
Sugar in everything→A small piece of gud (not for diabetes)
Late, heavy dinner→Early khichdi or soup

🧘 Lifestyle management in winter

Food works best with the right routine. These small habits keep weight, sugar and energy in balance through winter.

🚶30–45 min activityWalk in the sun or do yoga daily
💧Warm waterYou still need 7–8 glasses, even if not thirsty
😴Sleep by 10:307–8 hours of sleep
📵Screen-free dinnerEat slowly, without the phone
⚖️Weigh once a weekWinter weight gain is easy to miss
🧘5 min breathingMorning or before bed, to calm stress
Please note: This is a sample plan for healthy adults. If you have diabetes, BP, kidney or thyroid problems, are pregnant, or have a food allergy, ask for a personal plan – don't follow this as it is.
