How to verify an Ayurveda doctor

🔎

Check the registration

Ayurveda doctors are registered with their State Board of Indian Medicine and the national register kept by NCISM.

📄

Look at the prescription

A valid prescription shows the doctor's name, degree and registration number.

❓

Just ask

You can always ask the clinic for the doctor's registration details before your call.
